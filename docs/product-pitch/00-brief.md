## 3. Positioning

- One line: *Ruime appartementen aan de piste in Saalbach-Hinterglemm, van Nederlandse eigenaren die er zelf ook skiën.*
- Three attributes: **ruim** (space for 6 to 8), **dichtbij** (walk to the lift), **eerlijk** (clear prices, no surprises, Dutch contact).
- The gap competitors leave: big platforms (Belvilla, Chalet.nl, Sunweb) sell inventory; small owner sites are dated and hide prices. A small brand that shows price per week, distance to lift in metres, and a real host wins on trust.

## 4. Audience

- **Families**: two parents + 2 to 4 kids, school holidays only (Kerst, Voorjaar), care about beginner slopes, ski school, a bath, a dishwasher, a drying room.
- **Friend groups**: 6 to 8 adults, January or March, care about après-ski distance, big table, sauna, parking.
- Both: book Sept to Nov for the winter, compare 3 to 5 options, message before booking, want an answer within a day.
