## What everyone does the same

- Search widget first: dates (or arrival Saturday) + party size, then a results grid.
- Cards = photo, name, village, score, "vanaf" price; amenities as a short list.
- Discount badges and urgency ("boek vóór december", "-25%") on all platforms.
- Reviews as the main trust device (Trustpilot or per-property 1–10 scores); nobody shows a host face.
- Distance to lift/piste is the top selling line ("aan de piste", "150 m van skilift"), but rarely in a consistent facts row.
- Pricing is inconsistent: per night, per week, per person, per unit, "+extra kosten" — the guest must do the maths.
- Cookie walls / consent overlays before any content.

## Gaps a small honest brand can own

1. **One transparent price per week per apartment**, all-in (cleaning, linen, tourist tax), with a simple season table — no per-person maths, no fake discounts.
2. **Named Dutch hosts with a face and a WhatsApp number**, answering in NL within a stated time — Huisjetehuur promises this, nobody delivers it well.
3. **A fixed facts row on every apartment**: slaapt X · Y slaapkamers · Z m² · N m naar lift · N m naar centrum/supermarkt · parkeren · skiberging — the affiliate portal proves guests filter on this.
4. **Hinterglemm-only local depth**: which lift, which bakery, where the ski school meets, drive time from Utrecht, kerst/voorjaarsvakantie dates — platforms cannot go this deep.
5. **Honest inquiry flow instead of a fake "live" widget**: dates + party size → personal offer within 24 h, with a visible "vrij / bezet" calendar per apartment so people don't ask for booked weeks.
